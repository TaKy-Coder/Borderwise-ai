# BorderWise AI — 4-minute demo script

**Demo setup:** double-click `START_BORDERWISE.bat` (or run `py server.py`), use fictional values only, and keep the assistant in local demo mode unless the optional model API has already been tested. The figures below are illustrative and do not represent verified living costs or a real FX quote.

## 0:00–0:25 — Introduce the problem
"International students may need to plan tuition, arrival expenses, monthly living costs and money transfers across two currencies. BorderWise organizes these decisions in one place. It doesn't move money or pretend estimates are verified facts."

## 0:25–1:35 — Budget planner
Open **Budget Planner**. Use the prefilled demonstration scenario or input fictional figures. Click **Calculate my plan**. Point out total cost, monthly burn, funding gap and BDT-equivalent gap. Open calculation details and show that the FX rate is explicitly described as user-entered, not live. Change the FX assumption and recalculate to show sensitivity.

## 1:35–2:35 — Compare transfer quotes
Open **Transfer Comparison**. Keep a fixed total BDT budget and compare two fictional quotes with different fees and rates. Explain that BorderWise ranks by estimated recipient amount, not by brand, and that the output excludes possible intermediary/receiving-bank charges. Show the warning and optional JSON download.

## 2:35–3:10 — Credit and readiness
Open **Credit & Readiness**. Show the checklist about checking eligibility, interest, fees, credit reporting and payment responsibilities. Emphasize the agent does not offer a guaranteed credit-score outcome or country-specific eligibility claim.

## 3:10–3:40 — Ask the agent and show safety
Open **Ask BorderWise** and ask: "What are the current proof-of-funds requirements for Germany?" The local response states it cannot verify current rules and directs the user to official authorities. In local mode, this helper uses rules-based responses rather than a generative model. Ask about transfers; it explains the quote-comparison workflow and warns against sharing credentials.

## 3:40–4:00 — Close
Open **Audit log**. Show an event entry without prompts or amounts. Close with: "BorderWise turns a vague cross-border money problem into inspectable calculations, clear assumptions and safer next steps." 

## Capture notes
- Record a 1080p screen capture, hide API keys and terminal secrets.
- Keep the optional video under five minutes, as required by the event brief.
- Do not state or imply that a provider quote, exchange rate, credit result, or immigration requirement is live/verified.
